Questions & Answers

Frequently asked questions

Does Genova IPTV provide channels or playlists?

No. Genova IPTV is a player only and ships with no preloaded provider or channel (aside from fictional demo data). You connect your own legal M3U/M3U8 playlists or compatible streaming services.

Which platforms are available now and what comes next?

Genova IPTV is available right now on Google Play for Android Phones, Android Tablets, and Android TV / Google TV. Next in line is iPhone (iOS), followed by Apple TV (tvOS), iPad (iPadOS), and Desktop (macOS first, then Windows and Linux).

Does one subscription work on both my Android phone and Android TV?

Yes. A single Genova Premium subscription billed through Google Play (€2.99/month or €19.99/year after a 7-day free trial) unlocks playback on all Android phones, tablets, and Android TV devices linked to your Google account.

Are my playlists and credentials sent to your servers?

Never. Your playlists, server credentials, favorites, and watch history remain stored locally on your device. No user account is required, and Genova is 100% ad-free.
